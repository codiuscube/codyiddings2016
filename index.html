About

From Hanalei to the Guadalupe.

I grew up on Kauaʻi with humble beginnings, cut my teeth as an assistant art director at Surfer Magazine, designed products in California and New Zealand, and moved my family to New Braunfels, Texas in 2022.

I have a wonderful wife and three kids. We love to surf, play music, and spend time with family and friends. We're part of different communities in Texas — King's Community Church, NBCA Wildcats, NBYSA, Texas Gulf Surf Association — and excited to be part of the Texas community.

I'm a software designer and product leader with a passion for building products that help businesses grow. In that journey, I co-founded Makeshapes with two good friends — a team-learning platform used by enterprises around the world, where I lead product, security, and compliance. Running a small company means trading speed against budget against quality every single week.

I love to work with friends and local businesses to help others grow and succeed. Let's talk about how I can help you.
